## Becoming a maintainer

Ask in [discussion #19](https://github.com/orgs/openlint/discussions/19). Say which area you'd like to help maintain (specification, toolbox, or administration) and what you'd like to work on.

## Joining office hours

Office hours are weekly and open to anyone. Agendas are posted as issues in this repo, and recordings, summaries and transcripts go up on [openlint.org/meetings](https://openlint.org/meetings/). To be added to the invite, ask in [discussion #19](https://github.com/orgs/openlint/discussions/19) or email [info@openlint.org](mailto:info@openlint.org).
